# Project Overview


Human knowledge is constantly disappearing.

Every generation contains:

- expert knowledge,
- professional experience,
- practical lessons,
- discoveries,
- mistakes,
- solutions,
- personal insights.

However, when experienced individuals retire or pass away, much of this knowledge disappears with them.


Books preserve information.

Documents preserve facts.

Research papers preserve discoveries.


But they rarely preserve:

- how experts think,
- why decisions were made,
- lessons learned through experience,
- mistakes that shaped improvement,
- practical knowledge gained over decades.


NOVA explores how software engineering and artificial intelligence can create a digital infrastructure for preserving human expertise.

The goal is to transform individual experiences into a connected global knowledge network.

---

# Vision


> Every human mind contains a universe of knowledge. NOVA preserves those universes before they disappear.


NOVA aims to create a living archive where humanity's accumulated knowledge can be:

- captured,
- verified,
- connected,
- explored,
- and passed to future generations.


The platform focuses not only on storing information.

It focuses on preserving understanding.

---

# The Problem


Modern information systems are excellent at storing data.

However, valuable human knowledge often exists only inside people's experiences.


Examples:

A senior software engineer knows:
- The mistakes that cause large systems to fail.

A doctor knows:
- Practical patterns learned from years of patient care.

A teacher knows:
- The techniques that genuinely help students learn.

An entrepreneur knows:
- The decisions that created success or failure.

This knowledge is difficult to preserve because it is:

- unstructured,
- personal,
- experience-based,
- distributed across individuals.
